# Accessibility Compliance Abu Dhabi: What Your Digital Channels Must Meet

Your site loads fast. Your WhatsApp line replies in ten seconds. Then a customer with low vision zooms the browser to 175 percent and half your booking form vanishes behind a sticky header.

That is a compliance problem, not a design preference. The accessibility compliance Abu Dhabi digital service standards expect is written down in public documents, it references WCAG 2.1, and it applies to the channels you automate as much as the pages you publish.

## Key Takeaways

- Abu Dhabi's digital service standards include accessibility criteria, and the UAE design system 3.0 behind them references WCAG 2.1, with its latest release in 2023 (designsystem.gov.ae).
- Compliance is a design and code outcome: 175 percent zoom with no overlapping sections, icons at 24px or larger with supporting text for screen readers.
- Data protection is a separate layer split across three regimes: federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 for mainland, DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020, and ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021.
- WhatsApp automation adds its own list: a valid trade licence, a display-name review, and Meta's category-based conversation billing, which has been revised more than once.
- Published Dubai consultancy rates ($25-49 up to $150-199 per hour, minimum projects from $1,000+ to $50,000+) give you a budget frame; convert at the AED peg of roughly 3.67.

## What Accessibility Compliance Abu Dhabi Businesses Face on Digital Channels

Accessibility means a person with low vision or low hearing can finish the same task as everyone else on your site, your chat or your phone line. The UAE National Digital Accessibility Policy is what puts that expectation in writing nationally.

That policy supports digital transformation and service efficiency in the UAE while ensuring digital inclusivity for all segments of society, including people of determination, according to the [UAE design system](https://designsystem.gov.ae/guidelines/accessibility). Abu Dhabi's digital service standards carry accessibility criteria of their own.

Any channel that connects to or mirrors a government-adjacent service gets read against that bar. A TAMM-linked booking step or a Malaffi-adjacent clinic workflow is judged differently from a menu page.

The reference framework is also versioned, which catches people out. The design system referenced by these standards sits at version 3.0, and its latest release was in 2023 per designsystem.gov.ae.

## The UAE National Digital Accessibility Policy: Scope and What It Requires

The policy covers digital services and tools, so a booking page, a support portal and a chatbot all sit inside it. What it does not hand you is a certificate to file and forget.

The framing in the guidance is technical rather than administrative. When websites and web tools are designed and coded thoughtfully, they become accessible to people of determination, per the [National Digital Accessibility Policy](https://assets.u.ae/api/public/content/84ec9317f73249c8a2594d76705d3c46?v=8a2f9df6) and the design system that carries it. That puts the obligation on your build, your template and your chat widget.

Assessment runs component by component. Every guideline in the framework describes accessibility within its own structure, and every component added to the design system is built to comply, per designsystem.gov.ae.

So the accessibility compliance Abu Dhabi vendors claim should point at components, not at a single sign-off. Ask which parts of the channel were tested, not whether "the site is compliant".

## WCAG 2.1 and the UAE Design System 3.0: The Technical Baseline

Three specifics do most of the work here: WCAG 2.1 as the reference standard, 175 percent zoom tolerance, and a 24px floor on icons. All three come from the UAE design system.

Version 3.0 is built on accessibility-first principles, follows Web Accessibility Initiative guidance, and takes its references from WCAG 2.1, per designsystem.gov.ae. Your developer does not have to invent a standard. It already exists, in public, for free.

On zoom the instruction is blunt: set the browser default zoom to 175 percent and make sure no sections overlap each other. Test that on your booking form and your chat launcher, because overlays are where overlap usually surfaces first.

Icons must be at least 24px, with supporting text provided so screen readers can announce them. A chat bubble with no label is a dead end for anyone using a screen reader.

Because the latest version landed in 2023, a spec pulled from an older document should be rechecked before a vendor builds against it. Put the version number in the scope of work.

## Data Protection Rules That Sit Alongside Accessibility Compliance

Which data law applies depends on where your company is registered, not where your customer sits. Mainland, DIFC and ADGM run three separate regimes.

Mainland UAE companies fall under the federal Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

ADGM-registered companies in Abu Dhabi answer to the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021. DIFC-registered companies in Dubai fall under DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020.

That last one matters for automation specifically. The DIFC law covers automated decision-making, so a bot that decides something about a customer on its own, approving, rejecting, scoring or pricing, sits inside its scope.

There is no single "UAE data law". A vendor quoting one blanket policy has not checked which entity type you actually hold, and that is the same shortcut that produces a one-line accessibility claim. ## Accessible Media: Text, Images, Video and Voice in Customer Channels

Text, images, video and audio each carry their own requirement. A customer who cannot hear your voice note needs the same answer in text, and a customer who cannot see your chat bubbles needs it announced.

Content on a digital channel is consumed as text, images, video and more, and the design system treats each format separately. It also fixes a reference point for hearing: a person has hearing loss when they cannot hear as well as someone with normal hearing, which it puts at hearing thresholds of 20 dB or better in both ears (designsystem.gov.ae).

That threshold is the reason captions and transcripts stop being optional on a support channel. If your IVR or WhatsApp line sends voice replies, ship the text version into the same thread.

Voice-over use is addressed directly in the guidelines, so a channel built only on visual chat bubbles does not meet the standard by itself. Arabic adds a second layer, since labels, alt text and transcripts need the same care in Arabic as in English.

## Where Abu Dhabi's Own Platforms Already Set the Bar

Look at TAMM. Abu Dhabi's government services platform applies digital service standards that include accessibility criteria, and residents use it constantly, which is how they learn what "working properly" feels like.

Malaffi, the emirate's health information exchange, is the reference point clinics should hold in mind when they automate patient enquiries. A bot touching appointment or record workflows carries a heavier burden than a restaurant booking form.

Documentation gets requested at practical checkpoints too: ADDED licensing, and ICV certification when you bid for procurement work. Accessibility belongs in that folder next to your data protection position. The Department of Municipalities and Transport also publishes Abu Dhabi's [access standards code](https://www.dmt.gov.ae/-/media/Project/DMT/DMT/Codes/3-ADAccessStd_bookmarked.pdf), which tells you how seriously the emirate treats access generally.

## What to Check Before Automating WhatsApp or Chat Support

Four items go on the list before you sign a WhatsApp automation contract: trade licence, display name, billing model, and the human exit.

WhatsApp Business API onboarding in the UAE requires a valid trade licence and a display-name review before the number goes live. Your licence name and your display name need to line up, and that review runs on someone else's clock, not yours.

Meta bills conversations separately by category, marketing, utility, authentication and service, and it has revised that pricing more than once. Treat any flat per-message quote as provisional, and ask which categories your use case will actually trigger.

The fourth item is where accessibility and operations meet. An automated channel should never remove the human handoff for a customer who cannot finish the task in chat. A screen reader user stuck on an unlabelled button and an older customer who would rather talk need the same escape route, reachable in one step.

Confirm display-name timing and licence matching with the vendor directly rather than assuming it from a generic onboarding guide. ## FAQ

**Does Abu Dhabi have a specific law for website accessibility, or is it covered under the UAE National Digital Accessibility Policy?**
The national policy is the framing document, and it supports digital transformation and service efficiency while ensuring digital inclusivity for all segments of society, including people of determination (designsystem.gov.ae). Abu Dhabi's own digital service standards add accessibility criteria on top of it. Treat both as live rather than picking one.

**What is WCAG 2.1 and does an Abu Dhabi business have to follow it?**
WCAG 2.1 is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines set that the UAE design system 3.0 references, alongside Web Accessibility Initiative guidance (designsystem.gov.ae). If you build to the UAE design system, you are building to WCAG 2.1 by reference. Ask your developer which version they targeted and get it in the scope.

**Which data protection law applies to a WhatsApp bot run by a mainland company versus an ADGM company?**
A mainland company falls under the federal Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

An ADGM-registered company answers to the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021, a separate regime with its own obligations. A DIFC company falls under DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, which covers automated decision-making.

**What does WhatsApp Business API onboarding require for a UAE trade licence holder?**
A valid trade licence and a display-name review before the number goes live. Names should match the licence, and review timing is worth confirming with your vendor rather than a generic onboarding article.

**How much does accessibility and compliance work typically cost for a small business in the UAE?**
Published Dubai consultancy listings show hourly bands from $25-49 up to $150-199, with $50-99 the most common, and stated minimum project sizes from $1,000+ to $50,000+. Convert at the AED peg of roughly 3.67 before you compare quotes. Scope moves the total far more than the hourly rate does.

**Does TAMM apply accessibility standards that private businesses should match?**
TAMM is Abu Dhabi's government services platform, and its digital service standards include accessibility criteria. Private channels are not assessed against TAMM, but your customers use it daily and arrive expecting the same behaviour. Matching it is the safer commercial call, especially for clinics and anything linked to government workflows.
