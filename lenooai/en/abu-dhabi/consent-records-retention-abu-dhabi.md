# Records Retention UAE: Consent Rules for Abu Dhabi Customer Conversations

Records retention in the UAE is not one rule. It is a stack: federal tax law underneath, three separate data protection regimes beside it, and sector obligations layered on top. If you run a clinic, dealership, brokerage or restaurant in Abu Dhabi and you are about to point an AI assistant at your WhatsApp inbox, that stack decides what you may keep, how long you keep it, and what the customer has to be told first.

Most retention guides are written for accountants. This one is written for the person switching on automated chat.

## Key Takeaways

- Mainland, DIFC and ADGM companies sit under three distinct data protection laws: the federal Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, and the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021. There is no single "UAE data law".
- National Archives retention schedules govern government records, not the chat logs and customer files of a private business.
- Corporate tax records are generally held for 7 years and VAT records for 5 years after the end of the relevant tax period, with longer periods for capital assets and real estate.
- Employee records are typically kept 7 years after someone leaves, stretching to 10 years where a workplace injury claim was involved.
- WhatsApp Business API onboarding in the UAE needs a valid trade licence and a display-name review before a single automated message goes out.

## Which UAE data law actually governs your customer conversations

There is no single "UAE data law". Your registration decides which of three regimes applies: federal PDPL for mainland companies, DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020 for DIFC entities, and the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 for ADGM entities.

That distinction matters the moment a bot starts making decisions. DIFC's law specifically covers automated decision-making, so a DIFC brokerage that lets an assistant score, route or reject enquiries is doing something its regime names directly. A mainland clinic under the [federal PDPL](https://lenooai.com/blog/uae-pdpl) has a different set of obligations around lawful basis and data subject rights.

Getting this wrong is not a filing error. It changes your consent wording, your deletion policy and your exposure, and the [penalties under the PDPL](https://lenooai.com/blog/pdpl-penalties-uae) attach to the entity, not the vendor. A [practitioner overview of the region's data protection regimes](https://ca.practicallaw.thomsonreuters.com/w-046-4420?transitionType=Default&contextData=(sc.Default)) is a reasonable starting point, but check your own trade licence before you assume which law you sit under.

One company, two licences, two regimes. That happens more often than owners expect.

## Records retention UAE: how long you must keep customer and business documents

Seven years is the number to plan around for most business records, with five years for VAT and longer periods for assets. The Federal Tax Authority has publicly [reminded businesses to retain their records properly](https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/pr.28082025.aspx), and penalties follow from gaps, not just from errors.

Commonly cited retention guidance for UAE businesses sets the periods out like this:

| Record type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Corporate tax records | 7 years |
| VAT records | 5 years after the end of the related tax period |
| General records and documents | At least 7 years following the end of the relevant tax period |
| Capital assets, such as machinery | 10 years from the end of the tax period |
| Real estate documents | At least 15 years |
| Employee records | 7 years after the employee leaves |
| Employee records with an injury claim | Up to 10 years after the claim is resolved |
| Contracts, purchase orders, leases | At least 7 years after the contract ends |

A worked example from that same guidance: a tax invoice for a supply in April 2018 relates to the January to December 2018 tax period and should be held until 31 December 2023. The clock runs from the period, not the document date. That single detail catches out a lot of finance teams.

Where does a WhatsApp thread sit in that table? If the conversation contains a quote, an order confirmation or a payment instruction, it is a business record and inherits the underlying retention period. Casual enquiry chatter is not, and holding it forever is a liability rather than an asset.

## National Archives schedules vs what a private business must keep

National Archives retention schedules apply to government records. They are not the rulebook for a private clinic's patient enquiries, a dealership's test-drive requests or a restaurant's booking logs.

Owners search for them because the phrase sounds official, then build a policy around an obligation they never had. Your obligations come from tax law, your employment records, your contracts and your sector regulator. That is a narrower and far more practical list.

Sector variance sits on top of the general rule. Financial institutions commonly face 7-year requirements, and the [Central Bank's own rulebook on retention periods](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/article-2-period-retention-records) is the binding reference for licensed entities. Healthcare providers are generally held to 10 years or more, which is why a clinic cannot copy a restaurant's policy.

Write down which category each record falls into before you choose a deletion schedule. A single default of "keep everything" fails both directions: it breaks minimisation under your data law and it still leaves gaps where a longer sector period applied.

## Consent before you automate WhatsApp and support conversations

What has to be in place before an AI assistant answers your first customer message? A valid trade licence and a completed display-name review, since WhatsApp Business API onboarding in the UAE requires both. Automation without that is not a shortcut, it is a blocked account.

Meta bills conversations by category: marketing, utility, authentication and service. That pricing has been revised more than once, so quoting fixed per-message rates to customers or to your own board is a mistake. Model the categories, not a rate card that changes.

Then there is the part most setups skip. Disclosure. Customers should know they are talking to an automated system, and DIFC entities running automated decision-making inside chat have to account for their regime's explicit coverage of that activity.

**What the setup should not do:**

- Enrol customers into automated conversations without telling them, or without a way to reach a human.
- Retain full conversation transcripts with no defined deletion point.
- Push raw chat content into a model provider that keeps it. A [zero data retention arrangement](https://lenooai.com/blog/zero-data-retention-llm) with your AI vendor is the baseline, not an upgrade.
- Move Emirati customer data offshore without checking your [cross-border transfer position](https://lenooai.com/blog/cross-border-data-transfer-uae) first.
- Treat a consent checkbox collected for marketing as consent for automated processing. Those are different purposes, and [consent under UAE rules](https://lenooai.com/blog/consent-ai-data-collection-uae) is purpose-bound.

Deletion points are the piece owners find hardest. Pick a period per conversation type, write it into the system, and let it run.

## Abu Dhabi specifics: licensing bodies and sector data systems

Abu Dhabi adds its own layer on top of the federal picture. ADDED issues mainland licences, while ADGM, Masdar City and KEZAD run separate frameworks with their own rules, and TAMM is the government services platform you will meet for licensing and compliance touchpoints.

Clinics carry the heaviest load here. If you are connected to Malaffi, the emirate's health information exchange, patient data handling sits alongside your own retention practice rather than replacing it, and the [Department of Health's published requirements](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/FB7CE4A318974DE2B955F3FC4E3D7A72.ashx) govern the clinical side. Any chatbot touching symptoms, appointments or results falls under [healthcare AI rules in the UAE](https://lenooai.com/blog/ai-healthcare-regulation-uae) too.

Suppliers bidding into government and semi-government procurement have another reason to care. ICV certification brings its own record-keeping expectations, and an audit that finds missing documentation costs score points.

For everyone else, the practical version is short. Confirm your licensing body, confirm your data regime, set retention periods per record type, and make sure your [chat automation in Abu Dhabi](https://lenooai.com/blog/chatbot-development-abu-dhabi) is built around those periods instead of against them. ## FAQ

**How long do businesses need to keep customer records in the UAE?**
Most records are retained at least 7 years following the end of the relevant tax period, per widely cited retention guidance, with VAT records at 5 years after the related period ends. Customer conversations that contain quotes, orders or payment instructions inherit the retention period of the transaction they document.

**Do National Archives retention rules apply to private businesses in Abu Dhabi?**
No. National Archives retention schedules cover government records. A private clinic, dealership, brokerage or restaurant sets its retention practice from tax law, employment records, contracts and its own sector regulator instead.

**Is DIFC data law the same as UAE federal data protection law?**
They are separate. Mainland companies fall under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, DIFC-registered companies under DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, and ADGM companies under the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021.

DIFC's law also covers automated decision-making specifically, which matters if a bot scores or routes enquiries.

**What consent do I need before automating WhatsApp customer chats in the UAE?**
Customers should be told they are interacting with an automated system and given a route to a human, and consent collected for one purpose such as marketing does not cover automated processing. DIFC entities also need to account for their regime's treatment of automated decision-making. Set a deletion point for transcripts at the same time.

**How long should VAT and tax records be kept in the UAE?**
Retention guidance puts VAT records at 5 years after the end of the tax period they relate to, and corporate tax records at 7 years. Records for capital assets such as machinery run to 10 years, and real estate documents to at least 15 years.

**Do healthcare providers in Abu Dhabi have longer records retention requirements than other sectors?**
Generally yes. Healthcare providers are commonly held to 10-year-plus retention, while financial institutions typically face 7 years. Clinics connected to Malaffi also handle health data under the emirate's health information exchange arrangements, which sits alongside their own retention duties.

**What licence do I need before using the WhatsApp Business API in the UAE?**
A valid trade licence, plus a display-name review, before automation can start. In Abu Dhabi that means an ADDED mainland licence or a licence from ADGM, Masdar City or KEZAD depending on where you are registered.
