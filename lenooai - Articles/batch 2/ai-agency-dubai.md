## Define Your Use Case Before You Brief Any Agency

Name the workflow before you name the agency. Customer support, lead qualification, appointment booking, internal document handling, outbound calling: pick one and describe it in a paragraph. Vague briefs produce vague proposals, and vague proposals produce contracts you cannot enforce at go-live.

Then list your existing tools: CRM, ERP, WhatsApp Business setup, calendar, phone system. Integration complexity drives cost and timeline far more than the choice of AI model, and no honest agency will quote seriously without seeing this list.

Finally, set a measurable success metric before the first sales call. Cost per resolved ticket. Lead response time in minutes.

Hours recovered per week per team member. Sales conversion on qualified leads. Without a metric agreed in writing, "the system works" becomes a matter of opinion the day it goes live.

## How to Read an AI Proposal Without a Technical Background

You do not need to code to read a proposal properly. You need to check for a few things the pitch decks skip.

A real scope document names the exact integration points, identifies the specific data the system will touch, states who owns that data, and explains what happens to it when the contract ends. A feature list is not a scope.

Watch for two red flags. First, no mention of team training or knowledge transfer. A system your team cannot operate becomes a liability the moment the agency walks out.

Second, no compliance statement. Any agent handling customer data must address Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL), and outbound AI calling adds Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024. Silence on either is a serious tell.

The green light to look for is a satisfaction or refund guarantee written into the contract, not offered verbally in the sales call. Words in the signed agreement are what count in Dubai.

A quote well below AED 10,000 is not a bargain. It usually means the agency has removed training, removed integration, or is reselling a generic tool with a thin wrapper. Ask what has been taken out of scope to hit the number, in writing.

## Compliance Questions to Ask Every Agency on Your Shortlist

Compliance in the UAE is specific, and any competent agency should answer the following without hesitation.

On PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021): how does the system store, process and delete personal data? Who is named as the data controller?

Does any customer data leave the UAE, and if so, under what mechanism? An agency that shrugs at these questions is not ready to handle your customers' information.

On outbound calling, Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024 came into force on 27 August 2024. Ask whether the agency holds TDRA prior approval, uses locally registered numbers, respects the 09:00 to 18:00 calling window and can perform Do Not Call Registry checks before dialling.

Fines are not theoretical: AED 50,000 for a first DNCR breach, AED 75,000 for a second, and AED 150,000 for a third. For voice projects, the [AI voice agents and phone automation in Dubai](/blog/ai-voice-agent-dubai/) guide walks through the operational side.

## Arabic Is Not a Checkbox: What Genuine Multilingual Delivery Requires

WhatsApp is the primary customer channel in the UAE, and your customers write in Arabic, English and Arabizi (Arabic in Latin letters, often mixed with English in the same message). A system that routes anything mixed-script to a human for translation is not bilingual. It is monolingual with a fallback.

Ask a direct question: is Arabic supported natively in the model, or retrofitted through a translation layer? Retrofitted translation adds latency and loses dialect nuance, and in customer conversations that nuance decides whether the exchange feels human or robotic.

Bilingual delivery also has to reach your team, not just your customers. If your operations lead works in Arabic, the training sessions, admin documentation and support conversations must all be available in Arabic too.

A system your team cannot troubleshoot in the language they think in will fail quietly. If your primary channel is WhatsApp, the specific playbook for [WhatsApp Business automation in Dubai](/blog/whatsapp-automation-dubai/) covers the channel considerations in more depth.

## Post-Launch Accountability: Who Is Responsible When Something Breaks

Go-live is not the finish line. It is the moment the real questions start.

Ask for a specific support window in the contract. Not "ongoing support available" but a named duration, a response-time SLA, and a named point of contact you can reach when the system misbehaves at 10pm on a Thursday.

Confirm who owns the trained model, the prompt configurations, and the workflow logic at the end of the engagement. Some agencies retain IP that leaves the client permanently dependent on one vendor.

Finally, ask about breaking changes. Model providers push updates that occasionally break production behaviour without warning.

Who is responsible when that happens? At whose cost? Get the answer before it happens, not during the outage.

## FAQ

### How do I know if an AI agency in Dubai has real delivery experience or is reselling off-the-shelf tools?

Ask to see a live client system, not a demo environment, and ask who wrote which parts. Resellers describe features; builders describe integration decisions and edge cases they hit in production. If every case study centres on a familiar SaaS logo, you are paying agency margin for a subscription you could buy directly.

### What PDPL questions should I ask before sharing customer data with an AI agency?

Ask where the data is stored, who is named as the data controller versus the processor, how deletion requests are handled, and whether any data leaves the UAE. Under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, you remain the controller of your customers' personal data, so any agency acting as processor must document its obligations in the contract.

### What is a realistic timeline from first briefing to a live AI system in Dubai?

Entry-tier single-channel automation runs four to six weeks from signed contract to go-live. Mid-tier CRM-integrated multi-channel systems run eight to sixteen weeks. Anything quoted at "a few days" is almost always a template deployment.

### Does the agency need to be physically based in Dubai?

A Dubai presence is not strictly required, but it matters for PDPL and telemarketing compliance advice grounded in local practice, and for sitting with your team during discovery and training. Remote agencies often miss UAE market context that a local team treats as default.

### What is the difference between an AI automation agency and a software development company?

A software development company writes bespoke code for a specification you hand them. An AI automation agency scopes the workflow, picks and configures the models, integrates existing tools and trains your team.

### What happens if the system does not perform as promised after launch?

Only what the contract says. This is why the written scope, measurable acceptance criterion, and any satisfaction or refund guarantee must all be in the signed agreement.
