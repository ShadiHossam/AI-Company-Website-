Two gaps this structure is built to close

The live site has 12 EN service pages, only 4 industry pages, and a blog — but no location pages and no comparison pages. That is where most commercial keyword work currently has nowhere to land.

/services/ai-training is anchored to the exact seed term that produced a noisy alphabet-soup keyword export dominated by gig-work and exam-prep searches. That SERP needs re-anchoring — see §2.6.

Hard rule: one keyword, one page. If a term already has an owner row, the new idea becomes a sub-keyword on that page or it dies. This is what stops ai chatbot dubai from being fought over by a blog post, a service page and a future location page at the same time.

1 · Funnel / intent layer

Four buckets. The test is not what the phrase looks like but what would satisfy this searcher: a lesson, a shortlist, a vendor, or a specific brand.

INF Informational · TOFU

Searcher is learning. Money is 3+ months away.

  • what is agentops
  • prompt injection attacks on chatbots
  • why ai agents degrade over time
  • uae pdpl chatbot requirements

COM Commercial investigation · MOFU

Comparing options, prices, approaches. Highest-converting, most underbuilt layer.

  • ai chatbot cost dubai
  • n8n vs make vs zapier
  • chatgpt enterprise vs custom ai agent
  • questions to ask ai automation agency

TRX Transactional / local · BOFU

Wants to hire someone, this month. Never gets a blog post.

  • ai company in dubai
  • hire ai developer dubai
  • ai automation agency near me
  • ai company in difc

NAV Navigational

Wants a named entity — own brand or a competitor/tool brand.

  • lenoo ai dubai
  • intercom fin pricing
  • zapier alternative uae

Recognisers. COM: vs, cost, pricing, alternative, best X for Y, is X worth it, review. TRX: a city or free zone in the phrase, or a hiring verb — hire, company, agency, services, developer, near me.

The intent misfire that produced 90% noise

The autocomplete dump was mostly a fifth, unusable category: job-seeker and student intent — mercor ai training pay, outlier ai $20 an hour, ai-900 exam questions. It looks commercial (money words, "training", "certification") but the searcher wants to be paid or certified, not to buy. Tag these rejected on sight.

2.1 · The distinction the business already hit

ai company in dubai vs ai for real estate agents dubai — both contain "dubai", neither is the same keyword type.

ai company in dubai: the searcher is shopping for a vendor. A blog post here converts badly and can't outrank a page with an address, a phone number and prices on it. Location page. No exceptions.

ai for real estate agents dubai: the searcher is a broker wondering whether this applies to them. They want proof and mechanics. Blog post, linking down to /industries/real-estate and up to the Lead & Sales Workflows pillar.

The tell: swap the geography out. If ai company in london is still a coherent vendor search, it's TRX. If ai for real estate agents london is still an education topic, it's INF.

2.2 · Location pages — the highest-value gap, currently zero built

  1. /ai-company-dubai
  2. /ai-automation-agency-abu-dhabi
  3. /ai-company-sharjah
  4. Free zone tier: /ai-company-difc, /ai-automation-dmcc, /ai-company-adgm, /ai-automation-jafza

Free-zone pages only earn their place if they say something a Dubai page can't — DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, ADGM's own regulations, DMCC/JAFZA licence-renewal specifics. If it would just be the Dubai page with a word changed, don't build it. Minimum bar: a real address or service area, two named local outcomes, AED pricing anchors, one local compliance detail.

2.3 · Comparison pages — also currently zero built

Tool vs custom build: chatgpt enterprise vs custom ai agent, intercom fin ai vs custom chatbot, manychat vs custom whatsapp agent — these pre-frame the sale.
Tool vs tool: n8n vs make vs zapier (already article #21 — move it to /compare/ once the directory exists).

Rule: never write a comparison where Lenoo AI isn't a legitimate third option on the page.

2.4 · Industry pages vs vertical blog posts

Only 4 industry pages exist (hospitality, logistics, real estate, retail). Articles exist with no matching industry page for law firms, schools, construction, manufacturing, clinics, insurance, accounting — and retail has a page with no matching article.

Industry page = the offer ("we do this for you, here's the price"). Vertical blog post = the education and workflow detail. Promote a vertical to a full industry page once it has two published articles or one closed client — never speculatively.

2.5 · Pillar vs supporting

Unchanged from current practice: one cornerstone per cluster, 5–9 supporting posts, all link up to the pillar, the pillar links down to all of them. Existing pillars: #3 AgentOps, #14 Guardrails, #17 Cost, #39 Lead Qualification, #45 Ticket Triage, #50 PDPL, #51 Arabic Chatbots, #57 Free Zone, #59 Arabic Gulf Automation.

2.6 · The /services/ai-training problem

That page targets a term whose search space is gig-workers (Mercor, Outlier, hourly-rate queries) and exam candidates (AI-900, Udemy). Ranking for the bare term brings zero buyers. Re-anchor to the buyer version:

  • corporate ai training dubai
  • ai training for employees uae
  • chatgpt training for teams dubai
  • ai upskilling program uae

Add ai training bare, ai training jobs and every certification term to the negative list (§7). Same audit applies to /services/vibe-coding and /services/prompt-engineering — keep for positioning, don't spend link equity trying to rank them.

Wrong-geography reject: any term naming a country with no UAE/GCC connection is rejected, not parked.

4 · Language / market tiering

Fold in, or make a new cluster?

Default is fold in. A 10th cluster only opens when all four are true:

  1. It supports a pillar plus at least 5 supporting articles — fewer than 6 total is a sub-topic, not a cluster.
  2. It answers a distinct buyer question, not a distinct topic. ("AI Governance" is a distinct buyer question. "AI Voice Agents" is a distinct topic but the same buyer question as Lead & Sales — folds in.)
  3. It maps to something Lenoo AI sells or wants to sell.
  4. Its articles create more internal links inside the cluster than out to existing ones.
  • Missing verticals (ecommerce, salons/gyms, car dealerships, travel agencies, recruitment agencies, events, pharmacies) — fold into Industry Verticals, which grows to ~17 articles.
  • Free zone / hyper-local terms — not a cluster. TRX, so they become location pages linked from the #57 pillar.
  • Tool comparisons — not a cluster. COM, so they become /compare/ pages, with a shared hub once there are 4+.
  • AI Governance & Accountability — a legitimate cluster #10 candidate. Clears all four tests (AI use policy, model risk register, human-in-the-loop sign-off, incident response, vendor due diligence). Currently split across AI Security & Trust and UAE Compliance & Data — split out once those two pass ~8 governance-flavoured pieces combined.

Use the competitor-gap rating, not a keyword tool's difficulty score — tool difficulty measures global backlinks; the gap rating measures whether UAE agencies have actually published on it, which is what determines whether you can rank in months instead of years. Volume is deliberately the lightest weight: a Low-competition 20/mo term outscores a High-competition 500/mo term, correctly, for a young domain.

Overrides (beat the score)

  1. High competition + no geography caps at Phase 3, whatever it scores. ai chatbot is unwinnable — take ai chatbot dubai instead.
  2. TRX terms jump one band. A location/service page scoring 14 still builds in Phase 1 — revenue pages outrank content pages in the build order.
  3. A pillar always ships before its supporting posts, even when a supporting post scores higher.
  4. Anything failing §7 is rejected regardless of score.

7 · Keyword sourcing filter

Run every raw export through this before a single term enters the sheet. Seven questions — one "no" on Q1–Q4 is an immediate reject.

  1. Could this searcher become a paying client? Not "is this related to AI" but "does this person have a budget and a business problem." Kills every job-seeker term and every student/certification term.
  2. Is it in a market Lenoo AI serves? UAE, GCC, or geo-neutral.
  3. Is it a real query a human would type? Autocomplete produces strings nobody searches — game titles, GPU model numbers, truncated fragments all fail this.
  4. Does it map to something Lenoo AI sells or credibly writes about? If nobody could write 1,500 honest words on it, it's out.
  5. Does it already have an owner page? If yes, it's a sub-keyword on that page, not a new row.
  6. Does the live SERP have an opening? Check page one for the UAE — thin content or forum threads mean an opening; four established agencies with strong pages mean back off.
  7. Does the intent survive the swap test? Replace the city with another city. Stays commercial → TRX, money page. Stays educational → INF, blog post. Stops making sense → the geography was autocomplete noise.

Better seeds

The alphabet-soup method is fine — the seed was not. Re-run a–z / 0–9 suffix expansion on seeds that already carry a buyer, a product, or a place: ai agent, ai automation dubai, whatsapp bot, ai chatbot for, ai voice agent, automate + business function, and ذكاء اصطناعي / بوت واتساب for the Arabic side. ai training carried only a topic, so autocomplete filled the gap with whoever else uses that phrase — gig workers and exam candidates.

8 · Weekly sorting routine

  1. Dump new keywords into a staging tab.
  2. Run §7 — delete rejects outright, no "maybe" pile.
  3. Tag survivors: intent, geo, lang, cluster, page type.
  4. Score with §6, apply the overrides.
  5. Check §0 for an existing owner page — fold in or open a new row.
  6. Anything at 16+ goes into the build queue, TRX first.
