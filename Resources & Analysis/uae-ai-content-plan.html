How to run this plan

  1. Publish by phase. Phase 1 = the fastest UAE wins (low competition, high intent). Do these first, ~2 per week.
  2. Build clusters, not scattered posts. Publish each cluster's pillar first, then its supporting articles, and link them to each other. Google rewards the topical depth.
  3. Validate volume before big bets. The bands here are estimates. Confirm the real UAE numbers in Google Keyword Planner (location = UAE), and glance at page one live — if it is thin or foreign-only, that is your opening.
  4. Put the main keyword in the H1, the URL and the first two sentences; work the sub-keywords into H2s and body naturally.
  5. Never fabricate proof. Use real screenshots, real AED figures and real outcomes. That authenticity is both your ranking edge and your legal safety.
