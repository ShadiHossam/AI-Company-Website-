Lenoo AI SEO Audit

28 Oct 2026 · live crawl of all 652 sitemap URLs · Lighthouse lab runs · Supabase blog_posts · GSC exports (Performance to 20 Oct, Indexing to 16 Oct)

Summary

The technical base is in good shape: every page returns 200, canonicals and hreflang are clean, and schema is on every page. What holds the site back is authority and internal linking, not crawl errors. Search shows no brand presence for "Lenoo AI", and the 409 blog posts send almost no links to the service pages that are supposed to win commercial UAE searches. The largest new risk is the publishing queue: 368 of the 686 scheduled English posts follow one template repeated across industries, which is the pattern Google's scaled-content policy targets.

From GSC: weekly clicks fell from 20 to 6 between early September and mid October while impressions held near 800 a week. Average position slipped from 12.3 to 23.8. In the UAE the site sits at position 34 with 0.8% CTR.

Findings that block growth

Critical

The site has almost no authority in the UAE

Evidence
A web search for "Lenoo AI" Dubai returns nothing about the company. The homepage Organization schema has no sameAs. UAE average position is 34; "custom chatbot development uae" ranks around 74 and "ai automation agency" around 85. Competitors that do rank (iMPLEMENTAi, n8nera, QuantumFind, Soluvide, Altus Tech) are on LinkedIn, DesignRush, GoodFirms, Clutch and the Botpress experts directory.
Why it matters
On-page work is already above the level of most competitors. Without outside signals, Google has no reason to rank a new domain for commercial terms.
Fix
Create a Google Business Profile for the Business Bay office and a LinkedIn company page, then add both to sameAs. List the company on DesignRush, GoodFirms, Clutch and Botpress experts with the same name, address and phone (+971 50 195 1590). Then work on links from UAE business media and chambers. I can write the profile copy and outreach list.
High

Blog posts don't link to the pages that sell

Evidence
Of 117 English service and industry pages, the median has 7 internal links pointing at it. /services/ai-automation/dubai, /services/ai-automation/abu-dhabi, /services/ai-agents/development and /services/ai-automation/business have 3 each and 0 from any blog post. /services/ai-chatbot-development has 7. 62 sitemap pages have only 1 or 2 internal links.
Fix
Add 1 to 3 contextual links from each relevant post to its matching service page, with descriptive anchors (for example a WhatsApp post links to the WhatsApp automation service). A re-runnable script in the style of scripts/link-citations can do this across Supabase. Replace the five "Learn More" links on the homepage with descriptive text.
High

The publishing queue carries scaled-content risk

Evidence
686 EN posts are queued, one a day until 13 Sep 2028. 368 are in the "Industry Verticals" category. Whole families repeat one topic across 10 to 14 industries: what-to-automate-first (14), ai-automation-cost (14), ai-automation-mistakes (14), chatbot-vs-voice-agent (14), automated-reporting (13). 67 queued titles closely match a live post, for example ai-agency-ajman vs ai-agency-dubai. The median queued body is 11.0k characters vs 13.6k for live posts. Only 13 Arabic posts are queued.
Why it matters
Google's spam policies name scaled content made mainly to rank as abuse, and the helpful-content signal applies site-wide. A slow drip does not change how the pages look once they're all live.
Fix
Merge each template family into one strong guide with industry sections, keep a variant only where the industry needs different content, and drop the near-duplicates. Move the posts that support service pages and the commercial UAE topics to the front of the queue.
High

Page-one articles that get no clicks

Evidence
From GSC, 3 months: /blog/system-prompt-security 293 impressions at position 5.6, 0 clicks. /blog/whatsapp-ai-agent 93 at 6.9, /blog/voice-platform-comparison 74 at 6.4, /blog/saudi-dialects 58 at 6.6, /blog/tdra-telemarketing-rules 54 at 6.5, all 0 clicks. Close to the top of page one: /industries/hospitality (13.9), /industries/insurance (15.4), /services/arabic-ai/chatbots (18.7).
Fix
Rewrite the titles and descriptions to answer the searched question directly, and put a two-sentence answer at the top of each article. Add internal links into the near-page-one pages from the strongest related posts.
High

Mobile pages are slow in lab tests

Evidence
Lighthouse mobile: hospitality 45 (LCP 3.9 s, TBT 1.7 s), chatbot development 49 (LCP 3.7 s, TBT 2.1 s), system-prompt-security 58 (LCP 3.2 s). Desktop scores are 85 to 95. Most main-thread time goes to style and layout (2.7 s) and rendering (1.3 s), not to JavaScript; DOM size is small (518 to 607 nodes). The homepage mobile run failed twice with "no first paint", although a screenshot renders normally. Hero images are hotlinked from Unsplash and Pexels, which sets third-party cookies (best-practices score 78).
Caveat
These are lab runs on a laptop. The PageSpeed API quota was used up, so there's no field data yet. Confirm with the GSC Core Web Vitals report.
Fix
Find the CSS cost (large blur or backdrop-filter layers, animated gradients, the reading progress bar), host hero images yourself in AVIF or WebP with fixed sizes, and preload the LCP image.
Medium

Googlebot mostly gets uncached pages

Evidence
On a full crawl, 604 of 652 pages were a cache MISS and 47 had EXPIRED. Cold median TTFB was 1.49 s, p90 1.97 s and max 5.19 s (/ar/services/ai-agents/use-cases). A second request is a HIT at about 0.5 s. Edge cache lifetime is 30 minutes for posts and 60 minutes for pages, so long-tail pages expire before Googlebot returns. A load test of 40 parallel uncached requests all returned 200 (average 1.6 s), so there's no outage risk today.
Fix
Add a Cloudflare cache rule with a longer edge lifetime (about a day) for public HTML, with a purge on publish and on admin edits. Alternatively, prerender the static marketing pages.

Risks that could pull the site down

  • Templated posts going live daily: the main ranking risk over the next year (see above).
  • One origin server: o2switch has returned 500s and 503s before, and short edge caching sends most crawler traffic to it.
  • Hotlinked images: if Pexels or Unsplash change their hotlink rules, hero and article images break site-wide.
  • Staging leak: guarded today (noindex header, meta and Disallow). Keep checking after each deploy.

Checked and fine: the scheduled posts stay hidden until their date (canary returns 404 and is not in the sitemap); the fake case study is gone (/case-studies returns 404); contact details on every page match info@lenooai.com and +971 50 195 1590; SSL is valid to 11 Dec 2026 with HSTS, Brotli and HTTP/2.
